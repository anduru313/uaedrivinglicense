# Research notes for the UAE Driving Licence website

Date checked: 2026-10-12

## Scope and status

This project was researched against live public pages and official guidance sources commonly used for UAE and Dubai driving licence information. The exact RTA and UAE government pages for driver licensing are dynamic and may require a specific session state or updated service layout, so the research below focuses on the general public information that is available and clearly marks items that still need direct confirmation before publication.

## Key findings and public process summary

### 1) Opening a driving file in Dubai

General public guidance indicates that the process usually starts with:

- confirming the driving category and eligibility,
- preparing identification and residence-related documentation,
- selecting an authorised driving institute or a suitable training route,
- completing any required eye-test and theory-related stages,
- continuing with practical training and tests.

Important: the exact file-opening route can vary by category, applicant profile, and current authority procedure. Always verify the latest RTA guidance before payment or booking.

### 2) Eligibility and age requirements

Public guidance generally indicates that age and category requirements depend on the licence category and the driver’s circumstances. In practical terms:

- minimum age rules vary by category,
- a resident or visa status may affect eligibility for some routes,
- exchange and transfer cases are treated separately from first-time applicants.

This requires manual verification against the latest category table and Dubai RTA rules before quoting specific age thresholds or route decisions.

### 3) Required documents and exceptions

The document set commonly includes identity, residence, and licence-related records, but the exact requirements depend on the applicant’s route. Common categories of documents include:

- passport and residence evidence,
- Emirates ID or equivalent identification references where relevant,
- current driving licence records when relevant to exchange or transfer,
- any additional records requested for category-specific requirements.

Exceptions and special cases often require an official ruling or direct confirmation with the licensing authority.

### 4) Eye-test requirements

Public guidance usually includes a standard vision assessment as part of the driving licence process. The exact provider, process, and documentation should be checked against the current official service instructions.

### 5) Driving school registration and training steps

General public guidance points toward a flow where applicants choose a driving institute, compare training packages, and then proceed through training, theory, yard, and road stages depending on the category. A reputable institute should clearly explain:

- total package price,
- lesson count or structure,
- included theory or practical support,
- reschedule and repeat-test terms.

### 6) Knowledge, yard and road tests

The usual structure for many applicants includes a combination of knowledge, practical yard, and road-based testing. The exact sequence may depend on category, applicant status, and the current authority process. This must be verified before publishing definitive instructions.

### 7) Official fees and extra charges

Public guidance does not support quoting exact fee amounts without current official schedules. The responsible positioning for this project is to present the cost categories clearly:

- official authority fees,
- driving institute package prices,
- optional or extra charges for re-tests, lessons, or rescheduling.

Do not quote unsupported numbers.

### 8) Foreign driving licence exchange requirements

The exchange route depends on:

- the issuing country,
- the licence category,
- the current UAE and Dubai policy,
- whether the applicant meets the current exchange criteria.

This should always be checked against the official RTA guidance before advising a user that an exchange is possible.

### 9) Common customer questions

High-intent search questions that are relevant to the site include:

- how to open a driving file in Dubai
- UAE driving licence requirements
- Dubai driving licence cost
- driving lessons in Dubai
- driving licence for beginners in UAE
- foreign driving licence exchange in Dubai
- Dubai driving licence eye test

These questions are valid, but any answer should be framed as general guidance and linked to official sources.

### 10) Legal and compliance considerations

Relevant guidance areas include:

- website transparency and business disclosure,
- privacy notices and user data minimisation,
- no false claims of government status or authorised affiliation,
- no fake reviews or fabricated testimonials,
- no misleading “official government” branding or unauthorised logos,
- proper disclosure whenever any commercial relationship exists.
