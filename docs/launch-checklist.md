# Launch checklist

## Before publishing

- Replace all placeholders in the site configuration and environment variables.
- Confirm the real WhatsApp number and the contact email address.
- Confirm the business operator name and disclosure wording.
- Review the site copy for any unsupported claims or price figures.
- Check every page for accurate links and metadata.
- Verify the custom domain is connected and the canonical URLs match the live site.
- Submit the sitemap to Search Console.
- Review the privacy policy and terms for legal approval.

## Technical review

- Run lint, type-check, and build commands successfully.
- Test all routes and internal links.
- Test mobile and desktop layouts.
- Validate the floating WhatsApp button and CTA links.
- Confirm the sitemap and robots output are correct.
- Ensure no identity or sensitive document requests are being made on the public site.

## Legal and disclosure review

- Confirm no official government or driving-school affiliation is being implied.
- Review privacy and cookie wording if analytics is enabled.
- Check any paid or affiliate links before publishing.
- Confirm the legal operator and business status before public launch.

## Search and analytics

- Connect Google Search Console.
- Submit the sitemap.
- Configure Google Analytics only after consent documentation is in place.
- Confirm there are no personal data leaks in analytics events.

## Final launch

- Publish to the production host.
- Confirm the live homepage renders correctly.
- Test all key pages and CTAs from the final domain.
- Review the generated metadata and Open Graph output.
- Keep a record of the official source URLs and verification dates used for content decisions.
